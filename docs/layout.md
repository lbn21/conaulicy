# Layout

Every page follows one consistent layout. Read this before any UI change, and keep it up to date when a shared component changes.

## Every page: one structure, one visual layer

Every page, whatever its route (home, the hub, each subpage, `/ulice`, the static pages, the 404), is the same three sections:

1. **The page head** (`PageHead`, `web/components/page-head.tsx`): a band in the brand's surface tint (`surface-background`, wiśnia tint-95), the full width of the window, straight under the header, no gap. It holds, in this order, only what comes before the content: an address page's breadcrumbs (t400, below), the error summary, the caption, the h1 (on a module's subpage with the module's icon on its tile: the h1 a flex row, the tile one line tall, 40 px on desktop and 30 on a phone, centred on the first line's caps at any text size, a wrapped title hanging beside it; t366's C1), a lead (`govuk-body-l`) and the page's first action („Zmień adres”). The home page has the hero instead (below, „The home page”). `.app-band` in `web/app/site.scss`: full width by a clipped 100vmax shadow (nothing overflows), no margin above it: every route starts with a band, so the one `<main>` has no top padding (`govuk-!-padding-top-0`, `web/app/layout.tsx`; t368, before it the band was pulled up over GOV.UK's 20/40 with a negative margin); its last line's margin is the band's padding. **One spacing scale** for every head, GOV.UK tokens only, in one place (`.app-band`, `site.scss`; t373): the band's top padding 30/40 px (mobile/tablet), the caption (a subpage's street line) 10 above the h1 (GOV.UK's responsive spacing 2, where `govuk-caption-l` alone gives 5/0), the h1 20/30 above its next line (its own `govuk-heading-l` margin; „Zmień adres” on the address pages), the last line 20/30 above the band's bottom. On an address page the breadcrumbs are the band's first line, the band's top padding above them and the same token below (30/40), so they sit centred between the header and the next line (t400). The address page's head is its own (no caption: its locality is in the breadcrumbs), the same scale; the three subpages' heads are one (`AddressPage` with the module's caption, title and tile), only the module's words, icon and colour differ. `web/scripts/routes/head.mjs` checks the scale on all four pages, at 390 and 1280, on one place of each type, with the breadcrumbs the band's first line, centred, and the band 0 px below the header.
2. **The content**, on white, in GOV.UK's two-thirds column, built only from shared parts:
   - **Module colours and icons:** each module has a colour (`MODULE_COLOURS`, `web/lib/palette.ts`; [brand](brand.md#module-colours)) and a Lucide icon (`Icon`, `Tile`, `web/components/icon.tsx`: waste Trash2, water Droplet, power Zap, weather CloudSun; Clock for hours, TriangleAlert for failures and warnings, ChevronRight, MapPin, CalendarDays, FileText). `.app-mod--<id>` sets the module's colours as custom properties for everything inside it; a subpage's whole body is in its module's. An icon is 1.25em; it sits on its words by layout, never by an offset: in a button, badge, chip, alert title or item card a flex row (`align-items: center`), in a link that may wrap a flex row too, the icon one line tall at the first line's top (`height: 1lh`, so its centre is that line's: Atkinson's cap centre) and the wrapped words hanging beside it (t370). Icons are drawn on the server from the framework-free `lucide` package (no client JS), always `aria-hidden` and always beside words that say the same.
   - **Badges** (`Badge`, `web/components/badge.tsx`, `.app-badge`): a pill of 16 px bold text: muted (grey: „Odwołane”), the module's (its tint and ink: „Planowana”, „Planowane wyłączenie”, a count), alert (GOV.UK red with the warning icon: „Awaria”), strong (text colour: „Dzisiaj”, „Jutro”). Words always; colour never alone. Only when there is something to show: an empty state is never a badge.
   - **Chips** (`.app-chips`, `.app-chip`): a wrapping row of bordered pills: each window of hours its own (`TimeChips`, `web/components/rows/outage.tsx`, with the clock), the small bins, the home page's example addresses (links, the whole chip the target).
   - **Cards** (`.app-card`, shadcn/ui's Card): white, a subtle border and shadow, rounded, 16 px inside at the top and the bottom (any list's last item has no margin in a card); a summary list inside keeps GOV.UK's row borders but the last. `--accent`: a bar in the module's colour (`/smieci`'s next pickup). The timeline's day cards are the same look (`.app-timeline__card`).
   - **Item cards** (`.app-items`, `.app-item`, shadcn/ui's Item): a tile, the body, a chevron; the link's box the whole card's. 
   - **Empty states** (`.app-empty`): plain text in GOV.UK's secondary text colour, regular weight, no frame, pill, background or colour: „Na razie nic nie zaplanowano…”, a subpage with nothing to show, „Nie udało się sprawdzić…”, no waste dates.
   - **The failure banner** (`NowBanner`, `web/components/now-banner.tsx`, `.app-alert`): at the top of the hub's content, only while a failure is on at the address at the read (an outage marked emergency whose span holds the instant): GOV.UK red, „Teraz trwa awaria”, each cut with its hours as chips and „Szczegóły”. Read with the page; nothing appears later on its own. IMGW's warnings stay the cards above the timeline.
3. **The footer's band** (`web/components/site-footer.tsx`), unchanged.

Red is only for what is urgent (a failure, IMGW's level 3); a module's colour never is. Today and tomorrow with something stand out by the strong badge alone (every day's card the same thin grey border, t385); today's dot alone is filled; quieter things are quieter (an empty day, today and tomorrow too, is grey text, no card and no badge). No toasts, popups, or anything that appears or moves on its own; CSS only, no JS for looks.

## Address subpages: one structure

Every `/adres/<id>/<module>` page (`smieci`, `woda`, `prad`, and any new module; the weather has none) has the same structure, in this order, from one frame: `Subpage` (`web/components/module-page.tsx`, built on `AddressPage`, `web/components/place-page.tsx`), filled only from the module's fields (`Module`, `web/lib/modules.ts`); the module's `Details` is the body alone, from step 5:

1. The page head's band (`PageHead`, above), from `ModuleMain` (`web/components/module-page.tsx`): first the breadcrumbs (`PlaceBreadcrumbs`, `web/components/place-page.tsx`, built on the shared `Breadcrumbs`, below), then caption (place, street) + h1 (`Module.pageTitle`, the module's icon on its tile before it); before them, GOV.UK's error summary when the module has an error (`Module.error`: waste's house-number question).
2. „Zmień adres”: the band's last line (GOV.UK "Change" pattern).
4. The module's content (waste: the next pickup in an accent card, the next two dates in a card, the rest under „Pokaż więcej terminów”, bare in the details' indent, no card inside it; each date a heading (h3 `govuk-heading-s`, its date with its weekday) and its bins' list under it, GOV.UK's visible section break (`--m`) between dates, at every width: a summary list's key column wrapped „poniedziałek, 30 / listopada” from tablet up. A bin row is a flex row, the marks centred on the name, sized in em (48, 40 px beside 19 px text, 20 beside 16), so they grow with the text; outages, water and power alike: a day each, a card per outage with its summary list, each window of hours a chip, „od 8:00 do 14:00” (`fromTo`, `hoursRow` in `web/lib/outage-view.ts`), a value longer than 160 characters (PGE's house numbers) folded into a `govuk-details` „Pokaż całą listę”, then „Inne komunikaty”; nothing to show: the empty state). Water and power share one body (`OutageDetails`, `web/components/outage-page.tsx`), drawn from one shape (`OutagePage`, `web/lib/outage-view.ts`) that each fills from its own data (`waterPage`, `web/components/water-page.tsx`; `powerPage`, `web/lib/power-module.ts`): water's rows are „Godziny” and „Gdzie”; power's „Godziny”, „Rodzaj” (a badge: „Awaria” alert, „Planowane wyłączenie” the module's; revoked, a muted „Odwołane” and the kind in words with PGE's note), „Gdzie” (TERYT names) and „Numery domów według PGE” (verbatim). Power has no calendar, AI note or other notices.
5. The AI-read note: `AiReadNote` (`web/components/subpage-parts.tsx`), GOV.UK's warning text. Wording per source: `UNVERIFIED_NOTE` (`web/lib/schedule-notes.ts`) for the gmina's schedule, `waterAiNote` (`web/lib/water-module.ts`) for the water posts, naming the source of the cuts shown (the gmina's, MZGKiM's or both).
6. The actions (C2–C4 of t366, Jakub's call, t369): `CalendarBlock` (`web/components/subpage-parts.tsx`), a `section` under a visible h2 `govuk-heading-m` „Zapisz terminy” (`ACTIONS_HEADING`), after the dates they act on. Then GOV.UK's `govuk-button-group` of secondary link buttons (`govuk-button govuk-button--secondary`, no green primary: the page's answer is its dates, not an action), side by side from tablet, stacked full width on a phone: on `/smieci` „Harmonogram do druku (PDF)” first (the file icon, a plain `<a>` to the file), then the module's „Dodaj … do kalendarza” (the calendar icon, its ICS file). No hint under them. Directly under the group, a closed `govuk-details` „Jak dodać i sprawdzić, czy się udało?” with the shared `CALENDAR_STEPS`: how to add it on an iPhone and on Android, what to check and try, and Google Calendar on a computer; a browser never knows whether the import worked, so the copy never says it did. All of it lives only in the component, the same on every page. Its `event` prop and properties (and the PDF's, `file`) are what `web/components/web-analytics.tsx` sends on a click: waste `calendar_add` with `zone` and `source`, `pdf_open` with `zone`; water `water_calendar_add` with `place` (the place id). Shown only when the calendar file has at least one event (water: `waterCalendarHref`, `web/lib/water-ics.ts`; waste: a zone with upcoming dates); power has none.
7. The page's one source block, at the bottom: `SourceInset`, on the one source note every page shares (`SourceNote`, `web/components/subpage-parts.tsx`, t373: GOV.UK's inset text, `govuk-body-s app-quiet`): „Źródło: ‹`Module.sources`, joined with ;›.” (`sourceLine`, `web/lib/hub.ts`), then, for a live source (water, power), its freshness line on its own line, 10 px under the source: „Bez zmian od 13.11, 17:45.” (`freshnessLine`, from the module's `changedAt`: when the source's data last actually changed, written by the cron only when its content changes; DD.MM, HH:MM in Warsaw, joined by a no-break space), and under it how often its source is checked („Sprawdzamy co 15 minut.”, water „co 4 godziny”: `cadenceLine`, `web/lib/cadence.ts`). The waste subpage has its source line only („Harmonogram … z …”). A check time (`checkedAt`) is never rendered. Static: no script touches it (t416).

With no calendar file, or nothing to show, the rest keeps the same order. A module with no data (`load` → null) shows steps 1–3, then the hub's one line, „Nie udało się sprawdzić ‹`Module.subject`›.” (`unchecked`, `web/lib/hub.ts`), as the empty state (`app-empty govuk-body`). New subpages reuse these shared components instead of re-creating them. `web/components/subpages.test.ts` renders `/smieci` and `/woda` through `Subpage`, checks their order, the no-data state, and that both action blocks have the same markup but for `/smieci`'s PDF.

Metadata, one builder for every module (`subpageMetadata`, `web/components/module-page.tsx`): the title „‹h1› – ‹place›”, `Module.description`, and `noindex` unless `Module.indexed` says it may be a search result (waste only, as the address page: `indexedPlace`).

## Breadcrumbs

One component, `Breadcrumbs` (`web/components/breadcrumbs.tsx`), GOV.UK's breadcrumbs: every item a link, the page itself left out, `aria-label` „Ścieżka nawigacyjna”.

- Only on the address pages (`/adres/<id>` and its subpages): „Strona główna”, the locality, and on a module's subpage the address page (`PlaceBreadcrumbs`, `web/components/place-page.tsx`). Collapsed on mobile (`govuk-breadcrumbs--collapse-on-mobile`: below tablet only the first and last crumb, on one line), so a chevron never starts a line on its own.
- None anywhere else: `/o-projekcie`, `/prywatnosc`, `/dostepnosc`, `/ulice`, the home page and the 404. GOV.UK: „Do not use the breadcrumbs component on websites with a flat structure”, and the rest of the site is flat.
- The first line of the page head's band (`PageHead` `crumbs`, from `AddressPage`), inside `<main>` and the address pages' `govuk-width-container` (`web/app/adres/[id]/layout.tsx`), so the head is one band from the header down (t400, Jakub's decision): GOV.UK's standard breadcrumbs, not the inverse, on the band's tint (`surface-background`, `tint-95` in `web/lib/palette.ts`): links in GOV.UK's text colour 17.97 : 1, chevrons in its secondary text colour 8.29 : 1. GOV.UK places them before `<main>` so the skip link passes them; here they are in `<main>`, and the skip link's target is the heading block after them (below, Skip link), so it passes them all the same.
- One `<main>` for the whole site, the root layout's (`web/app/layout.tsx`), the landmark: Next.js keeps a page left by a client-side navigation in the document, hidden (Activity), so a `<main>` per page would repeat; a layout is never duplicated. `<main>` sits in no width container: each page holds its own `govuk-width-container` (the address pages' in `web/app/adres/[id]/layout.tsx`, the 404's in `web/app/not-found.tsx`; the home page's hero is outside one, the window's full width). A page never reaches out of `<main>` to change the layout (no `:has()` on a page's markup, no pathname class): a page left hidden is still in the document, so such a rule still matches on the next page (t398, the address page flush left after the home page). `web/scripts/routes/soft-nav.mjs` checks every width container centred and at most 960 px after client-side navigations from and to the home page, at 1280 and 1920.
- They come with the page, prerendered with it: no parallel-route slot (until t400 the layout's `@breadcrumbs` slot drew them before `<main>`).
- **Skip link** („Przejdź do treści”, `SkipLink`, `web/components/skip-link.tsx`, the body's first link): its target, `#main-content`, is the start of the page's heading block, `SkipTarget` (`web/components/skip-target.tsx`) with `tabindex="-1"`, as GOV.UK's target: in `PageHead` the street line (`govuk-caption-l`) when there is one, else the h1, so on an address page it comes right after the breadcrumbs and the skip link passes them; on the home page the hero's h1. Every page has one: every page has a `PageHead` or the hero. One id in the document: a page hidden by a client-side navigation (Activity) gives its target's id up (an effect's cleanup) and takes it back when shown. GOV.UK's `SkipLink` script finds its target once, when it starts, and the target here is each page's own (new after each navigation), so `SkipLink` finds it on each click and focuses it with GOV.UK Frontend's own `setFocus` and the `govuk-skip-link-focused-element` class, as GOV.UK's script does; that script is not started (`web/lib/govuk-frontend.ts`). With JavaScript off the browser moves focus to the target itself (`tabindex="-1"`).
- `check-html` asserts in every route's static HTML one `<main>` and one `#main-content`, not the `<main>`; `check-a11y` one visible `<main>`, one `#main-content` (visible, in `<main>`, `tabindex="-1"`) and no id twice on four representative routes; the routes check `skip-link` (`web/scripts/routes/skip-link.mjs`) presses Enter on the skip link on an address page, its waste subpage (full load, and after a client-side navigation) and the home page, at 390 and 1280: the focus on the one `#main-content`, the street line or h1, in the band after the breadcrumbs' `<nav>` (on the home page, its h1).
- Spacing: no margin above the trail (the band's top padding, 30/40 px), the same token below it (`.app-band > .govuk-breadcrumbs`, `site.scss`), so the gap from the header to the trail equals the gap from the trail to the next line. `breadcrumbs.test.ts` checks both use the same tokens.

`web/components/breadcrumbs.test.ts` renders the component and the address page's frame (the trail the band's first line); `web/scripts/check-warnings.mjs` checks on every route that they appear only on address pages, once, as the first line of `<main>`'s band; `web/scripts/routes/head.mjs` and `soft-nav.mjs` check the same in the browser, the band 0 px below the header, after full loads and client-side navigations (an address page, its subpage, its breadcrumbs back).

## Navigation

Every link to a page of the site is Next.js's `<Link>` (`next/link`): the header's logo, the breadcrumbs, the footer's links, the home page's examples and its link to `/ulice`, `/ulice`'s list, the hub's links to its subpages, the search's results, „Zmień adres”, the 404's link home, the links between the static pages and a module's own links to an address page (`Parts`, `web/components/parts.tsx`, by an href starting with `/`). A click is a client-side navigation: one document, the layout (header, footer, `<main>`) kept, only the page replaced.

A link to an id on the same page is a `<Link>` too, `FragmentLink` (`web/components/fragment-link.tsx`, no prefetch): the error summaries' links to their field and the skip link. A plain `<a href="#…">` makes a history entry the App Router does not know, so Back would show another page under the current URL; a `<Link>` pushes its own entry and scrolls to the id. GOV.UK's scripts keep their part: the error summary's focuses its field (and stops the navigation); the skip link focuses its target with GOV.UK's `setFocus` (above, Skip link).

A plain `<a>` stays only for another site, `mailto:` and a file the browser opens itself (the ICS and PDF links, `webcal:`, `/fonts/OFL.txt`); `web/components/internal-links.test.ts` holds every plain `<a>` to that, and the routes check `in-page` (`web/scripts/routes/in-page.mjs`) clicks the skip link, then the logo, then Back, and opens `#skad-to-wiemy` directly.

- **Static pages, plain 404 (t416):** every page and file is prerendered whole at `next build`, with its data (Caching, CLAUDE.md): Cache Components off, `dynamic = "error"` on the root layout, `generateStaticParams` and `dynamicParams = false` on every dynamic route (`/adres/[id]` and its subpages, the PDF, `woda.ics`, `/ics/[zoneId]`, `/podglad/imgw/[scenario]`), so only what the build made exists: any other URL, an unknown id or anything after an id that is no subpage or file, is the prerendered 404 from Vercel's routing layer, no function run and no cache entry per probe. A query string changes nothing (no page reads `searchParams`). No proxy. CI parses the build's route table (`web/scripts/check-routes-table.mjs`): only `/api/cron/zuk`, `/api/cron/import` and `/api/revalidate` run on request. A renamed place answers 308 for its page and everything under it, from the build's redirects (`web/next.config.ts`, from the build's snapshot); a retired place nothing replaced is a 404.
- **Prefetch:** Next.js's default for every link: a page in view is prefetched whole, from the static output, so a click shows it at once. No skeleton, no `<Suspense>`, no loading state anywhere.
- **Fresh after a change:** a page's data is one cache scope (`pageData`, `web/lib/page-cache.ts`), tagged with the page's own tag and every source it reads (dataset, imgw, pge, water). The zuk cron works out every page's view after each run and revalidates the own tag of each page whose digest changed (`web/lib/page-refresh.ts`, `status/page-digests.json`) with the "max" profile (stale served at once, the new page rendered in the background), every changed page in the run, then pre-warms at most 400 of them, the home page first (the rest are served stale once and re-rendered on their next visit); an import's POST `/api/revalidate` the dataset's, or deploys when the place set changed (`web/lib/dataset-sync.ts`). Back and Forward show the page as it was left.
- **Focus and scroll:** Next.js's route announcer (a polite region) says the new page's title; focus stays where it was, as Next.js leaves it: on the header's or footer's link, which stays, or on the body when the clicked link left with its page (the skip link is then the next Tab). The page scrolls to its top when its start is out of view (`<Link>`'s default `scroll`). No own focus code but the link to the search field (`FieldLink`, „Teraz w gminie”, below) and the skip link's (above).
- **State left on a page:** Next.js keeps up to 3 pages left by a navigation in the document, hidden (Activity), by design: two hubs visited in turn are both there, so their ids are there twice while both are kept (accepted); the checks look for an id twice within one page. The home page's search starts over on every visit by a link („Zmień adres”, the logo) and comes back as it was on Back: its column is keyed by `useRouter().bfcacheId` (`SearchErrorProvider`, `web/components/address-search.tsx`). GOV.UK Frontend's JavaScript starts the components of each new page (`web/components/govuk-frontend.tsx`, on each new path).
- **The search's one place:** „Szukaj” opens it with `useRouter().push`; the place's page comes with the navigation, its h1 streamed after the address route's App Shell. `router.prefetch` fetches only the App Shell, so it is not used.

## Headings

- One h1 per page.
- Module and section headings are h2 (on the hub `govuk-heading-l`, on other pages `govuk-heading-m`); no skipped levels.
- When a list needs a section heading for screen readers but not on screen, it gets a visually hidden h2 (`govuk-visually-hidden`), as the pickup list on `/smieci` (`web/components/pickup-schedule.tsx`).

## Typography

One rule: **19 px for content, 16 px for metadata and chrome, bold only for headings and keys.** Only GOV.UK Frontend v6's type scale and classes, no new sizes or weights (`19` is 19/25 at every width, `16` 16/20, `24` 21→24, `36` 27→36, `48` 32→48, `80` 53→80).

| Role | Class | Size, weight |
|---|---|---|
| h1 on the home page | `govuk-heading-xl` | 48 (32 on a phone), bold |
| h1 everywhere else, the 404 included | `govuk-heading-l` | 36 (27), bold |
| Caption over a subpage's h1 | `govuk-caption-l` | 24 (21), regular, secondary |
| Lead paragraph (home, static pages, `/ulice`) | `govuk-body-l` | 24 (21), regular |
| Section h2 | `govuk-heading-m` | 24 (21), bold |
| Sub-section and card title, h3 | `govuk-heading-s` | 19, bold |
| Content: body, lists, summary lists, insets, details, warning text, buttons, the search field, its hint and suggestions | GOV.UK's default | 19, regular (keys and warning text bold) |
| Inside a hub timeline card | `govuk-body-s`, the card rule in `site.scss` | 16, regular; keys bold |
| Small print: the subpages' source block („Źródło: …”, the freshness line and the cadence, `SourceInset`), the hub's note (the freshness line) and IMGW's required wording (hub) | `govuk-body-s app-quiet` (GOV.UK's 16, our secondary colour; never `govuk-!-font-size-16`, whose `!important` line-height would beat the 1.5 below) | 16/24, regular, secondary |
| Chrome: breadcrumbs (GOV.UK's own 16) and the footer (`govuk-!-font-size-16` on `.govuk-footer`, a deviation, below) | | 16, regular |

On purpose, so nobody "fixes" them:

- The hub's notice inset (`Status`, `web/components/parts.tsx`) is 19: it is content („Nie mamy jeszcze harmonogramu wywozu śmieci dla tej wsi…”, „Nie udało się sprawdzić…”), not metadata.
- „Wystaw je do 6:00 rano” is 16 inside a hub card and 19 on `/smieci`, as its siblings on each page.
- The `/smieci` hero (`NextPickup`, `web/components/pickup-schedule.tsx`, `aria-hidden`, so visual only) has three tiers: the label („Najbliższy odbiór”, „Dziś”) as its h3's 19 bold, the day's number at 80 bold (GOV.UK's size for a large number, set bold as the panel title), and the month and weekday at 24 regular (`govuk-!-font-size-24 govuk-!-font-weight-regular`).
- `/woda`'s day sections are `govuk-heading-m`, GOV.UK's own size; so is a hub warning card's event (h2, as a section's), its band „Stopień N z 3” `govuk-body-s` bold.

## Indexing

- Indexed, and in the sitemap (`web/app/sitemap.ts`): the static pages, and for each place with data its hub and `/smieci`.
- `noindex` and not in the sitemap: `/woda` and `/prad` (their content lasts days and is mostly empty), PDFs, ICS files (`X-Robots-Tag`, `web/next.config.ts`), the IMGW preview, and places without a waste schedule. A query string is canonical to the page without it.
- `web/scripts/check-indexing.mjs` checks both lists, and with `--expect=index` (by hand, `docs/launch.md`) that every URL in the sitemap is indexable (200, no noindex, canonical itself).

## Checks and tests: structure, not copy

The route, HTML, PDF and IMGW checks (`web/scripts/**`) and the rendered-markup tests (`web/components/*.test.ts`) check structure and data, never the page's words: an h1 with text, a description, the title's pattern („‹h1› – conaulicy.pl”, the home page's and a place's with a locality), the order of the page's parts by their markup, dates and times from the data. No check bans a word. A test that needs a page's words reads them from their source (`MODULES`, `unchecked`, `IMGW_ATTRIBUTION`…), so changing copy never breaks a test. The library tests (`web/lib/*.test.ts`) still check which message a state gives, as the behaviour under test.

## Site-wide: the same job, the same component

Across the whole site the same job always uses the same component and style:

- one button style per action (a page's actions, the PDF and adding to a calendar: secondary `govuk-button`s in GOV.UK's button group, `CalendarBlock`; no primary on an address page);
- one place for what is coming soon (the hub's timeline, `Timeline` in `web/components/timeline.tsx`): a card per day, a row per item with „Szczegóły” to its subpage (IMGW's warnings are the cards above it); each module's source and freshness on its subpage;
- one freshness line for the live sources (weather, power, water), the source note's last lines: when the data last changed, never when it was checked (`freshnessLine`, `web/lib/hub.ts`): on a subpage its own („Bez zmian od 13.11, 17:45.”), on the hub and the home panel each live source by name („Bez zmian od: pogoda 13.11, 17:45; woda 12.11, 08:00.”), then how often they are checked (`cadenceLine`, `web/lib/cadence.ts`: „Sprawdzamy co 4 godziny.” on `/woda`; on the hub „Sprawdzamy co 15 minut: pogoda, prąd; co 4 godziny: woda.”); the waste schedule its source line only. Static, no client script (t416).
- Link text says where it goes (e.g. „Wszystkie odbiory śmieci”); never a bare „Kliknij tutaj”. The timeline's „Szczegóły” is GOV.UK's summary-list action, with its visually hidden suffix („Szczegóły o odbiorze śmieci, wtorek, 10 listopada”), as GOV.UK's „Change”.

## No loading states (t416)

Every page is prerendered whole: no `<Suspense>` hole, no skeleton, no view transition, no pulse, nothing that moves after the first paint. The dates around the word are absolute, with their weekday.

The search lives only on the home page: its field is in the static shell, the same element before and after hydration (`web/components/address-search.tsx`). Wybierz swój.” and the links); none is plain text, never an error („Nie znaleźliśmy „…””, h2, then what to try). Own CSS cap 2.7 → 2.77 KB gzip for it.
- **The head's street line (t373, Jakub):** a subpage's caption (`govuk-caption-l`) sits 10 px above its h1 (GOV.UK's responsive spacing 2) instead of GOV.UK's 5/0, so the street line reads apart from the module's title; one rule, `.app-band > .govuk-caption-l`.
